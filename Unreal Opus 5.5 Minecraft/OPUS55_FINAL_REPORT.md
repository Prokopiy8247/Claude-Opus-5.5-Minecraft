# Opus 5.5 final report: Opus 5.5 Minecraft Unreal

## Overview

A single-player recreation of Minecraft Java Edition (26.2 era) in **Unreal Engine 5.8.2 / C++**, built inside the
project (`Unreal_Minecraft.uproject` at the root of the Unreal project folder). The game is a custom voxel
engine (chunk streaming, lighting, meshing, custom scene proxies) plus Minecraft's gameplay systems, with a
stylized-realistic look: Lumen GI, virtual shadow maps, procedural PBR block textures, and Blender-authored models for
every mob, the bosses, vehicles, end crystals and hero held items. Everything is original (procedural textures, sounds
and UI art, Blender-modelled meshes); no Mojang code or assets.

Launch → title screen → Singleplayer → Create New World starts a **Creative** world by default; Survival, Adventure and
Spectator are available (world options, `/gamemode`, F3+F4).

## Major implemented features

- **Voxel world**: 16×16 chunks, Y −64..320, worker-thread generation and meshing, sky/block light with smooth lighting
  and AO, 7 material layers (opaque, cutout, translucent, water, lava, animated Nether portal, parallax End portal),
  render distance 12 at ~110–140 fps (1280×720), save/load of all three dimensions.
- **Content scale**: 898 blocks (5097 states, 3318 static models), 1261 items, 870 crafting + 186 smelting +
  194 stonecutting + 30 brewing + 10 smithing recipes, 89 mob types, 65 biomes, 19 structure types, 657 procedural
  texture layers.
- **Overworld**: climate-noise terrain with 55 biomes (incl. the 26.2 Sulfur Caves, Lush Caves, Deep Dark, Pale Garden,
  Cherry Grove), aquifers, ores, trees, villages and other structures; day/night with moon phases and stars; rain and
  thunder; fluids with Minecraft's source rules.
- **Nether**: five biomes, lava ocean, glowstone, fortresses and bastions, Nether mobs; portals ignite, link with ÷8
  coordinates and keep the inventory.
- **End**: main island, obsidian pillars with caged end crystals, Ender Dragon with boss bar and fight manager, exit
  portal and gateways, outer islands.
- **Creative**: Java-style catalogue with tabs, search, survival-inventory tab and destroy slot; flight; instant break;
  pick block; `/summon` for any mob (NoAI/Silent tags) and bosses.
- **Survival**: health, hunger/saturation, air, XP, armour, mining times with tool tiers and durability, drops, death
  and respawn; Minecraft-scaled HUD.
- **Stations**: crafting table, furnace/blast furnace/smoker, chest, enchanting table, brewing stand, anvil, grindstone,
  stonecutter, smithing table, loom, cartography, beacon, hopper, dispenser, crafter, shulker box, villager trading.
- **Entities and combat**: melee, projectiles (bow, crossbow, trident, spear, mace), TNT and explosions with craters,
  boats, minecarts, item frames, XP orbs, falling blocks; Ender Dragon and Wither bosses.
- **Art pipeline**: Blender MCP (`blender_unreal`, port 9878, `UnrealMinecraft.blend`) → FBX → commandlet import:
  731 static meshes (all mob rig parts, player arm, boat, minecart, item frame, end crystal, 12 held-item props with tier
  materials); procedural texture synthesis; HLSL materials generated by the editor module.
- **Audio / VFX / UI**: procedural sound synthesis; particles for breaking, explosions, portals, flames and weather;
  Slate UI with Minecraft GUI scale (title, world select, options, pause, HUD, all inventories, chat, F3 debug).
- **Automation**: `-Opus55Tour` validation tour (38 scenes with per-scene render stats, TNT, portal ignition and travel,
  save) and `-Opus55LoadCheck` reload verification.

## Known bugs and limitations

- Many gameplay systems (survival mining/hunger/death loop, recipe execution, enchanting/brewing logic, redstone beyond
  TNT priming, full dragon fight, Wither fight, End portal activation via strongholds, riding) are implemented but were
  not exercised end-to-end in this session; they are marked PARTIAL.
- End cities are not generated; armour has no worn 3D visual.
- Functional blocks (chests, torches, lanterns, campfires, beds, stations) are detailed voxel models with procedural
  textures rather than Blender meshes.
- Only the player travels between dimensions.
- The first frames after a big `/time set` jump can look washed out for ~2 s; terrain can blink once when shaders finish
  compiling on a first launch.
- Textures are procedural originals: recognisable families, not pixel-exact Minecraft art.

## Build and run

- Build and open: `Launch-Windows.bat` or `Launch-macOS.command` (Unreal Engine 5.8.x).
- Content rebuild: `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <uproject> -run=pythonscript -script=<abs>/Tools/content/build_content.py`.
- Play: open `Unreal_Minecraft.uproject` and press Play, or
  `UnrealEditor.exe <uproject> -game -windowed -ResX=1600 -ResY=900` (add `-Opus55AutoStart` to skip the title screen).
- Validate: `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <uproject> -game -Opus55Tour -Opus55World=Tour -Opus55Seed=12345`.
- Saves: `Saved/Opus55Worlds/<world>/`. Details: `DEVELOPMENT.md`.
